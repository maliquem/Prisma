libpyrowave-shared-1.dll, embedded in Prisma for the PyroWave codec of Share screen.
Built unmodified from https://github.com/Themaister/pyrowave (commit 7fe0e2667cd3076b8b2bf04bcb80bc8d691b800b)
with Granite (https://github.com/Themaister/Granite, commit 53090429539bf3404250d18fe58db971f0907c04),
volk (commit 47cddf7ed97b94118a08aacb548a411188e016cc) and Vulkan-Headers (commit 6802bb4733b63ed5efd3adb308a6c885ef180ea1),
by tools/build_pyrowave.ps1 in the Prisma repository. The license notice of each part follows.
